Privacy Policy

T9 Still Alive Dialer · Last updated 27 July 2026

This app collects nothing, transmits nothing and stores nothing off your device. It holds no INTERNET permission, so it has no technical means of sending data anywhere, to us or to anyone else.

What the app accesses

PermissionWhyWhere it goes
READ_CONTACTS To build the T9 search index — names are converted to digit sequences so typing 343 can find אמא. Held in memory while the app is open. Discarded when it closes.
CALL_PHONE To place a call when you tap a result. Handed straight to Android's telephony system, which passes it to your normal dialer.

What is stored on your device

  • Your settings — theme, icon, tap behaviour, active keypad layout.
  • A per-contact call counter, used only to order the frequent-contact tiles.
  • Your chosen default number for contacts that have more than one.
  • Any keypad layout files you import.

All of this lives in the app's private storage on your phone. Deleting the app removes it. You can clear the counters and defaults yourself from the settings panel.

What is not done

  • No data is collected, uploaded, sold or shared.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or advertising.
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no cloud sync.
  • No third-party libraries or SDKs of any kind.
  • Contacts are never copied, exported or written to.

Calls

The app is not a dialer and does not take over your calls. It never requests the default-dialer role and never implements InCallService. Calls are issued as a standard ACTION_CALL intent, so your phone's own dialer handles them — along with its call log and, on devices that support it, its call recording. This app has no access to any of that, and no visibility into your call history.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date. The app's source is public, so any change in behaviour can be verified independently.
